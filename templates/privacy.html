Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

SwytchAI ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: Name, username, email address, and organization name when you sign up.

Device Credentials: Hostnames, IP addresses, usernames, and passwords for network devices you add to SwytchAI. These are encrypted and stored securely.

Usage Data: Actions performed within the platform (config pulls, changes, approvals) are logged for audit purposes.

Payment Information: Credit card and billing details are processed and stored by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the SwytchAI Service
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To maintain audit logs for compliance and security
  • To send important service notifications
  • To improve the Service based on usage patterns

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Bcrypt password hashing
  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Session timeouts and rate limiting
  • Role-based access control (RBAC)
  • Complete audit trail of all actions
  • Organizational data isolation (multi-tenant architecture)

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your data. We only share information with:

  • Stripe — for payment processing only
  • Law enforcement — only when required by law

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, all data including device credentials, configurations, and audit logs are permanently deleted within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data at any time through the dashboard
  • Update or correct your information
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

7. Cookies

SwytchAI uses session cookies to maintain your login state. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

8. Children's Privacy

SwytchAI is not intended for use by individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify users of significant changes via email or in-app notification.

10. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact us at privacy@swytchai.net
